The problem they give you

They will not say “ATM.” They describe this:

Given: A walk-up machine reads a card, checks a PIN, and pays cash. The tray may not be able to make the amount even if the bank says yes.

Find: Machine states, a bank port, and a hopper chain. Authorize, payout, capture or reverse. Never store PIN.

Tiny example: $30 requested, only 50s left — refuse and do not debit. Fake bank declines; hoppers do not move.

Name of the tool

A session authenticates a card; an Account port performs banking operations; a CashDispenser chain pays out notes; the ATM machine has a hardware state.

Why this tool

ATM is hardware + domain + a chain of dispensers.

Approach

  1. Draw machine states and the BankPort methods.
  2. Implement withdraw with authorize/payout/capture/reverse.
  3. Build a hopper chain with counts.

More notes if you want them

ATM is hardware + domain + a chain of dispensers. It tests ports (bank network), state (card in, pin, serving), and fail-fast on insufficient cash in the tray vs insufficient funds.

Use when: Hardware + ports, chain of responsibility, two-phase money.

Skip when: Do not design the entire card network (HLD).

Trap: Debiting before knowing cash can be made, with no reverse.
