The problem they give you

They will not say “Car rental.” They describe this:

Given: A fleet sits at stations. Customers book a type or a VIN for dates, pick up, and sometimes return elsewhere.

Find: Hotel booking plus location and vehicle lifecycle. Overlaps on a VIN are forbidden. Maintenance hides a car from search.

Tiny example: Two overlapping bookings fail. One-way return moves the car; search at the new station finds it.

Name of the tool

A fleet of Vehicles, Stations, and Reservations over DateRanges with a pricing policy and pickup/return that change vehicle status. Same bones as hotel booking, plus location.

Why this tool

If you already did hotel, this is the 'add location and vehicle state' variant.

Approach

  1. Reuse DateRange.overlaps.
  2. Decide type-hold vs vin-hold.
  3. Implement search/book/pickup/return.
