The problem they give you

They will not say “Payment and wallet.” They describe this:

Given: User.balance += from six places. Retry credits twice. transfer(a,b) deadlocks. Amounts are floats.

Find: Wallet aggregate, ledger, Money, idempotent debit/credit. Ordered locks on transfer. Cards are a port.

Tiny example: 10 balance, 100 threads debit 1 — ten successes, end at 0. A↔B opposite transfers do not deadlock.

Why this tool

Money designs fail on races, retries, and missing ledgers.

Approach

  1. Money value, no floats.
  2. Invariant balance == sum(entries) (or last snapshot + deltas).
  3. Idempotency on every mutating command.

More notes if you want them

Money designs fail on races, retries, and missing ledgers. If you can keep sum(ledger)==balance and survive a double submit, you can do ATM, checkout, and Splitwise settlements.

Use when: Wallets, gift cards, in-app balances, as a port for checkout.

Skip when: A single 'amount' field on User with += from everywhere.

Trap: Double credit on retry.
