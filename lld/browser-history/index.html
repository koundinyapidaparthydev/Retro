Browser history

CORE

Definition

A History object is two stacks (back, forward) plus a current URL. visit clears forward; back/forward move the pointer. Optional persist and search are ports.

Why it exists

Small, finishable, and a clean encapsulation test.

Approach

  1. Two stacks + current.
  2. visit clears forward.
  3. Do not expose the stacks.
Time O(1) visit/back/forwardSpace O(n) pages in stacks (+ log if kept)
