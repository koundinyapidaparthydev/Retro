Fail fast

CORE

The problem they give you

They will not say “Fail fast.” They describe this:

Given: A booking with guestCount -1 dies at payment. Config missing PORT boots and crashes on first request. Money.of silently clamps negatives to 0.

Find: Reject bad shape at the boundary and broken invariants in the constructor. Do not store half-valid objects.

Tiny example: Money rejects non-positive amounts. park validates before occupy. Missing collaborator fails startup.

Name of the tool

Reject bad input and broken invariants at the earliest boundary so you never store or act on a half-valid object.

Why this tool

A booking with a negative guest count that fails at payment time is a worse design than a constructor that refused it.

Approach

  1. Validate shape and ranges at the application boundary before you build domain objects.
  2. Put invariant checks in constructors and mutators; throw or return a Result immediately.
  3. Do not 'fix' bad input silently (clamping a negative quantity to 0) unless the product says so.

You say this first

I wouldn't name the pattern first. I'd say the problem fail fast solves, then the three types. Reject bad input and broken invariants at the earliest boundary so you never store or act on a half-valid object.

Shape: Validate shape and ranges at the application boundary before you build domain objects.. Don't: Reading legacy data you must still display — migrate or tolerate, then refuse new writes. Show a constructor that throws on empty plate or negative price. It takes ten seconds and looks senior.

More notes if you want them

A booking with a negative guest count that fails at payment time is a worse design than a constructor that refused it. Fail-fast keeps the rest of the system simple because it may assume the object is legal.

Use when: Constructors of values and aggregates.

Skip when: Reading legacy data you must still display — migrate or tolerate, then refuse new writes.

Trap: Catching exceptions and returning null.
