The problem they give you

They will not say “Library.” They describe this:

Given: Members borrow physical copies, not ISBNs. Due dates and fines depend on student vs faculty. Search is by title.

Find: Title vs Copy. Loan record plus a policy for limits and dues. Clock for fines.

Tiny example: Borrow/return/fine with a fixed clock. Reservation queue assigns the next returned copy.

Name of the tool

Catalog of titles, copies you can loan, members, and a loan policy for due dates and fines. The copy's status and the Loan record are the source of truth.

Why this tool

Library is a gentle aggregate: Book (title) vs Copy (item), Loan, Member.

Approach

  1. Split Title vs Copy on the diagram immediately.
  2. Implement borrow/return with a Clock.
  3. Enforce max-loans on Member via the policy, not a magic number in the service.
