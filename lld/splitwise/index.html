The problem they give you

They will not say “Splitwise.” They describe this:

Given: Friends log dinners. Equal, exact, or percent splits. Someone wants a simplify button. Money must not drift.

Find: Expense plus a split strategy. Ledger stays balanced. Simplify suggests payments and does not rewrite history.

Tiny example: Equal and exact on four users. Percent that is not 100 fails. Leftover cents assigned on purpose. No floats.

Name of the tool

Users, Groups, and Expenses that fan out into LedgerEntries. Balances are projections; simplify is a graph-min-cash-flow problem on those balances.

Why this tool

Splitwise tests money values, a ledger that must stay balanced, and an algorithm (simplify debts) that is not the source of truth.

Approach

  1. Money + currency check on every expense.
  2. Split.compute(total, participants) → map user→share, sum equals total.
  3. Update BalanceBook atomically with the expense.
