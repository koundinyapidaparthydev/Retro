Coffee machine

CORE

The problem they give you

They will not say “Coffee machine.” They describe this:

Given: The first sketch is Latte extends Espresso extends Beverage. Drinks are recipes over milk and espresso. Last milk can race.

Find: Recipe data, inventory consume, payment port. Add-ons as extras or a wrapper. No drink class tree.

Tiny example: Three recipes plus a mocha extra. New drink is a map. Two orders, last milk — one fails.

Name of the tool

Recipes consume Ingredients from an Inventory; a Payment port collects money; the machine is a small workflow (select, pay, brew). Composition, not a Beverage inheritance tree.

Why this tool

The classic 'inheritance vs composition' trap: Latte extends Coffee extends Beverage.

Approach

  1. Store recipes as data, not as classes per drink.
  2. Inventory.reserve/consume atomically.
  3. Addons adjust qty/price via decorator or a list of extras.
