Coffee machine

CORE

Definition

Recipes consume Ingredients from an Inventory; a Payment port collects money; the machine is a small workflow (select, pay, brew). Composition, not a Beverage inheritance tree.

Why it exists

The classic 'inheritance vs composition' trap: Latte extends Coffee extends Beverage.

Approach

  1. Store recipes as data, not as classes per drink.
  2. Inventory.reserve/consume atomically.
  3. Addons adjust qty/price via decorator or a list of extras.
