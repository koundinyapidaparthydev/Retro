Snake and ladder

CORE

Definition

A Board is a map of teleports; Players have a position; a Game loop rolls a Dice port and applies moves until a winning square.

Why it exists

A completeable game that still has ports (dice), entities (player), and a board as data.

Approach

  1. Board.apply(position) → after teleport.
  2. Dice.roll() port with a FakeDice in tests.
  3. Game.turn() is the unit of play.

Use when: Finishable LLD, ports for randomness, config-driven boards.
