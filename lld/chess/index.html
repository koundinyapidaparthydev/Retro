The problem they give you

They will not say “Chess.” They describe this:

Given: Two players move pieces on 8×8. A move that leaves you in check is illegal. They will ask about castling or a weak AI later.

Find: Piece.legalMoves. Game.apply is the only mutator. Clone or try/revert for self-check. Scope specials.

Tiny example: Rook, bishop, knight movement + apply + check. Undo via history. No 400-line switch in Game.

Name of the tool

A Board of Squares holds Pieces that compute legal moves; a Game owns turn, check, and history. Polymorphism lives on Piece, not in a 64×64 switch.

Why this tool

Chess is the polymorphism + rules interview.

Approach

  1. Piece interface with legalMoves and color.
  2. Board.get/set/inBounds; no game rules on Board beyond occupancy.
  3. Game.apply is the only mutator of record.
