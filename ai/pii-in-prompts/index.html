Do not paste the customer

CORE

The problem they give you

They will not say “Do not paste the customer.” They describe this:

Given: A support ticket has an email and a phone. Someone pastes it into the model to summarize.

Find: What do you strip before the model and before the log?

Tiny example: Replace the email and phone. Keep “adult user, billing issue.” The vendor is another copy.

Name of the tool

Prompts and logs are copies of the data. Emails, phones, and health text do not belong there raw.

Why this tool

You already felt this in healthcare-shaped interviews.

Approach

  1. Regex + allow-list fields before embed and before complete.
  2. Do not put raw transcripts in traces you keep forever.
  3. If the task needs the identifier, that is a different, reviewed path.
