The problem they give you

They will not say “Largest Rectangle in Histogram.” They describe this:

Given: A row of bars. Bar i has height h[i] and width 1. A rectangle must sit on the baseline and stay under the bars.

Find: The largest area of any such rectangle.

Tiny example: heights [2, 1, 5, 6, 2, 3] → 10 (the 5×2 block sitting on the 5 and the 6).

Name of the tool

For each bar, the largest rectangle with that bar as the shortest uses the nearest shorter bars on left and right as exclusive walls. One monotonic stack finds both.

Approach

  1. Append a 0-height sentinel.
  2. For i from 0 to n (inclusive, sentinel): while height[i] < height[stack.top], pop j, width = i - stack.top - 1, area = height[j] * width.
  3. Push i.

Whiteboard prompts

These are problem statements. The algorithm name is your answer, not the title of the question.

  • Largest rectangle under this histogram.
  • A binary matrix of 0/1. Largest rectangle of 1s.
  • What is the width for bar i once you know the nearest shorter walls?

You say this first

For each bar, the largest rectangle with that bar as the shortest uses the nearest shorter bars on left and right as exclusive walls. One monotonic stack finds both. I'd dry-run a tiny case first, then say the invariant and the complexity (O(n) time, O(n) space).

Offer the next notch: For i from 0 to n (inclusive, sentinel): while height[i] < height[stack.top], pop j, width = i - stack.top - 1, area = height[j] * width.. Tradeoff: Largest square (use DP on consecutive 1s — different recurrence). Draw one bar and its left/right smaller walls. Then say you do that for every bar via a stack.

More notes if you want them

This is the classic hard monotonic-stack problem and the engine inside 'maximal rectangle in a binary matrix' (treat each row as a histogram of consecutive ones). If you can explain the width = right[i] - left[i] - 1 picture, you own the problem. Brute force over all i,j pairs of bars is O(n²) and is the wrong finish.

Use when: Largest rectangle under a histogram.

Skip when: Largest square (use DP on consecutive 1s — different recurrence).

Trap: Width = i - j instead of i - newTop - 1.
