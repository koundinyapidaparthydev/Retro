The problem they give you

They will not say “Design trending topics.” They describe this:

Given: Millions of posts a minute, and the homepage wants “what is exploding right now.”

Find: v1: count recent mentions and surface a top list that is not just bots.

Tiny example: 200k events/s, 10-minute windows, one celebrity name is 30% of traffic.

Name of the tool

Detect what is spiking now: stream counts in windows, score against a baseline, fight manipulation, and serve a tiny hot list.

Why this tool

Trending is a stream-processing design with a serving cache.

Approach

  1. Ingest events; extract topics; filter junk.
  2. Update windowed counts and baselines keyed by topic+geo.
  3. Every T seconds, compute scores, take top N, publish snapshot.

You say this first

I'd treat this as a 45-minute HLD. First I lock v1 of trending topics: who uses it, read vs write, latency, what is v2. Detect what is spiking now: stream counts in windows, score against a baseline, fight manipulation, and serve a tiny hot list.

Then API, data, one write path and one read path. Deep dive: Ingest events; extract topics; filter junk.. Shorter windows: faster, noisier.

More notes if you want them

Trending is a stream-processing design with a serving cache. It teaches windows, sketches, and why raw count ≠ trend (California always has more tweets than Vermont).

Use when: Twitter trends, YouTube trending, news, retail 'what's hot.'

Skip when: Do not GROUP BY hashtag on OLTP every minute.

Trap: One global counter shard for all topics.
