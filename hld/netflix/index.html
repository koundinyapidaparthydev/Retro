Definition

Catalog + subscription + VOD playback at global scale: encoding, CDN/Open Connect-style, and personalization as a separate plane.

Why it exists

Unlike YouTube UGC, Netflix is a smaller catalog, heavier encoding, licensed DRM, and a CDN you may own (ISP appliances).

Approach

  1. Browse hits a cached personalized row API with fallbacks.
  2. Play: authorize geo/entitlement → license → player fetches segments from nearest cache.
  3. Preposition popular titles to ISP boxes before premieres.

More notes if you want them

Unlike YouTube UGC, Netflix is a smaller catalog, heavier encoding, licensed DRM, and a CDN you may own (ISP appliances). The design lesson is control of distribution and a browse experience that is not the player.

Use when: SVOD interviews, course platforms with DRM, 'Netflix vs YouTube' compare.

Skip when: Do not design user uploads unless they ask.

Trap: Personalized homepage uncacheable at the edge with no cohorting.
