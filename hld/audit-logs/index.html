Audit logs

NEXT

Definition

Append-only records of who did what to whom — for security, support, and compliance — separate from debug logs.

Why it exists

Money, admin, and privacy actions need a trail you can trust.

Approach

  1. Instrument privileged and data-changing APIs with a standard audit helper.
  2. Persist via outbox; ship a copy off-box.
  3. RBAC who can read audits; reading audits is audited.

You say this first

Append-only records of who did what to whom — for security, support, and compliance — separate from debug logs. I'd only add it if a requirement we wrote down actually needs it. Admin tools, payments, ACL changes, logins, deletes, exports.

Tradeoff: Sync audit in txn: stronger, slower writes.. Failure mode: Mutable audit table with UPDATE permission for the app role. For payments/Dropbox/admin, add an audit box with retention.

More notes if you want them

Money, admin, and privacy actions need a trail you can trust. Debug logs rotate in 7 days and get sampled. Audit logs are a product: complete, tamper-evident, retained, and searchable by actor and target.

Use when: Admin tools, payments, ACL changes, logins, deletes, exports.

Skip when: Do not treat debug stdout as the audit trail.

Trap: Mutable audit table with UPDATE permission for the app role.
