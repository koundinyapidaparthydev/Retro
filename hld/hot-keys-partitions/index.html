The problem they give you

They will not say “Hot keys, hot partitions, and thundering herds.” They describe this:

Given: One celebrity, one SKU, one cache key. The fleet is fine; one shard is at 100%.

Find: Find the hot key and split, cache, or isolate it — more boxes will not help.

Tiny example: user_id of a celebrity, Black Friday SKU, a thundering herd on expiry.

Name of the tool

When one key, shard, or cache entry absorbs traffic that your average-case math assumed was uniform — plus herds that stampede a backend.

Why this tool

Horizontal scale assumes a good partition key.

Approach

  1. Assume a power-law: 1% of keys may be 50%+ of traffic.
  2. Read-hot: multi-layer cache, replicate the key to all cache nodes, long TTL + stale-while-revalidate.
  3. Write-hot: shard the key (stripe counters), buffer in a queue, or accept approximate values.

More notes if you want them

Horizontal scale assumes a good partition key. Reality has celebrities, flash sales, and a single popular URL. Thundering herds turn a cache expire into a database outage. If you cannot name the hot-key plan, you have not finished the estimate.

Use when: Social graphs, flash sales, media, trending topics, multi-tenant SaaS with whale customers.

Skip when: Do not hash only on created_at for a write-heavy event table — the current bucket is always hot.

Trap: Using user_id of a celebrity as the only shard key for their inbound writes.
