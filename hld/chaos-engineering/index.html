HLD/Reliability

Chaos thinking

ADV

Definition

Deliberately inject failure (and latency) to verify that the design you drew actually survives — in staging first, then with a blast radius in prod.

Why it exists

HLD interviews sometimes ask 'how would you gain confidence?' Chaos is the answer beyond unit tests: kill an AZ, add 2s latency, expire certs.

Approach

  1. Write the hypothesis: 'if we kill one AZ, p99 stays < 300ms and error rate < 0.1%.'
  2. Inject one fault with a small blast radius and a hard stop.
  3. Watch SLOs, queues, and error budgets; abort if you are wrong.

You say this first

Deliberately inject failure (and latency) to verify that the design you drew actually survives — in staging first, then with a blast radius in prod. I'd only add it if a requirement we wrote down actually needs it. After you have SLOs, deploys, and multi-AZ — to verify them.

Tradeoff: Prod chaos: real confidence, real risk.. Failure mode: No abort switch. When they ask about multi-AZ, add 'we would game-day a failed AZ and measure RTO.'
