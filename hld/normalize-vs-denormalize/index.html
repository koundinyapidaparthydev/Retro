Normalize vs denormalize

CORE

The problem they give you

They will not say “Normalize vs denormalize.” They describe this:

Given: The feed join is five tables and 80ms. Product wants the card in 10ms.

Find: Decide what stays normalized for writes and what you copy for reads.

Tiny example: Post + author + like count on every card, 100:1 reads, author changes their name.

Name of the tool

Normalize to protect invariants and update-in-one-place. Denormalize to make a read cheap. The art is which copy is allowed to be stale.

Why this tool

Feeds, product pages, and chat previews are denormalized.

Approach

  1. Draw the read payload; decide which fields are snapshotted vs joined vs cached.
  2. Update-in-txn when the copy must not diverge (order line price).
  3. Update-async when lag is OK (display name on old posts).

Whiteboard prompts

These are problem statements. The algorithm name is your answer, not the title of the question.

  • Do you join at read or copy into the feed row?
  • What happens when the source field changes?

You say this first

Normalize to protect invariants and update-in-one-place. Denormalize to make a read cheap. The art is which copy is allowed to be stale. I'd only add it if a requirement we wrote down actually needs it. Normalize: financials, unique constraints, slowly changing dimensions.

Tradeoff: Normalized: correct writes, heavier reads.. Failure mode: Unbounded nested documents. For newsfeed, say 'denormalized feed rows, normalized user table, async name updates.'

More notes if you want them

Feeds, product pages, and chat previews are denormalized. Ledgers are not. Candidates either join ten tables on the read path or duplicate everything with no invalidation story. Interviews want a hybrid.

Use when: Normalize: financials, unique constraints, slowly changing dimensions.

Skip when: Do not denormalize a balance onto ten tables.

Trap: Unbounded nested documents.
