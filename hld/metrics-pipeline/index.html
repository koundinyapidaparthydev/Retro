Observability as a product (Datadog-like) or your internal pipeline. The design is ingest → buffer → aggregate → TSDB, plus the rule that user_id on a metric will melt you.

Use when: Internal observability, Datadog-like interviews, product telemetry.

Skip when: Do not use this path for business billing events (use the ad/ledger designs).

Trap: One Prometheus with local disk as the company store.
