Dead-letter queues

CORE

The problem they give you

They will not say “Dead-letter queues.” They describe this:

Given: One poison message crashes the consumer. The queue stops, and 200k good messages wait behind it.

Find: Decide retry budget, isolation of bad messages, and who gets paged.

Tiny example: Workers 50, a bad payload 0.01%, retry storm every 2s, business cannot lose money events.

Name of the tool

Where poison messages go after retries fail — so one bad payload does not block the partition or the queue forever.

Why this tool

At-least-once systems will see poison JSON, missing foreign keys, and downstream 500s.

Approach

  1. Set max retries with jitter; classify errors.
  2. On exhaust or poison, write to DLQ with reason, stack, and original headers.
  3. Alert on DLQ depth and oldest message, not only on process crashes.
