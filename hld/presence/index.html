The problem they give you

They will not say “Presence.” They describe this:

Given: Friend lists show “online” but the truth is a heartbeat that can lie after a crash.

Find: Decide how presence is stored, expired, and fan-out without N² polls.

Tiny example: Heartbeats every 15s, 5M online, a phone backgrounds and the socket dies quietly.

Name of the tool

Who is online, idle, or typing — ephemeral state with heartbeats, TTLs, and fan-out that must not become a write storm.

Why this tool

WhatsApp green dots look trivial and are a hot-key + fan-out problem.

Approach

  1. Heartbeat → refresh TTL; on create/expire emit a transition event.
  2. Watchers subscribe to topics for the users they currently view.
  3. Store sessions in Redis hashes; last_seen occasionally to SQL.

Who is online, idle, or typing — ephemeral state with heartbeats, TTLs, and fan-out that must not become a write storm. I'd only add it if a requirement we wrote down actually needs it. Chat, collab docs, multiplayer lobbies, support consoles.

Tradeoff: Short TTL: accurate offline, more heartbeats and false flaps on bad networks.. Failure mode: Presence rows in the primary DB. Separate heartbeat path from message path; they have different SLOs.

More notes if you want them

WhatsApp green dots look trivial and are a hot-key + fan-out problem. Presence is loss-tolerant and high-churn. Treat it as a specialized ephemeral store, not rows in Postgres updated every 5 seconds.

Use when: Chat, collab docs, multiplayer lobbies, support consoles.

Skip when: Do not write last_seen to SQL on every heartbeat at 10M DAU.

Trap: Presence rows in the primary DB.
