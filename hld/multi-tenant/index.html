Multi-tenant: silo vs pooled

NEXT

Definition

Silo gives each tenant their own stack. Pooled shares infrastructure with tenant keys everywhere. Most SaaS is pooled with silo for whales or compliance.

Why it exists

Tenant isolation is a security, noisy-neighbor, and cost problem.

Approach

  1. Put tenant_id in the primary key or as a mandatory filter (RLS).
  2. Authorize every request against the tenant in the token, not the URL alone.
  3. Quota CPU, QPS, storage, and expensive APIs per tenant.

More notes if you want them

Tenant isolation is a security, noisy-neighbor, and cost problem. Designs that forget tenant_id leak data. Designs that silo everyone cannot afford the product. Interviews expect a hybrid and a shard-by-tenant story.

Use when: Pooled: standard SaaS, consumer apps with 'accounts.'

Skip when: Do not silo thousands of tiny tenants on thousands of Postgres instances on day one.

Trap: Missing tenant filter on a new endpoint (IDOR).
