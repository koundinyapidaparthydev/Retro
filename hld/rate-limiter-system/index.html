Design a rate limiter

CORE

Definition

A distributed service that decides allow/deny (and remaining quota) at tens or hundreds of thousands of QPS without becoming the outage.

Why it exists

You already know token buckets.

Approach

  1. Gateway extracts identity; calls limiter (or Lua on Redis) atomically.
  2. On deny, 429 without hitting the app; on allow, decrement remaining.
  3. Replicate rule config; keep a local cache of policies.

I'd treat this as a 45-minute HLD. First I lock v1 of rate limiter: who uses it, read vs write, latency, what is v2. A distributed service that decides allow/deny (and remaining quota) at tens or hundreds of thousands of QPS without becoming the outage.

More notes if you want them

You already know token buckets. This design is about placement (gateway vs sidecar vs service), a shared counter store, hot keys, and fail-open vs fail-closed.

Use when: Public APIs, login, search, multi-tenant fairness, ticket drops.

Skip when: Do not put the limiter on the far side of a 30ms remote region for every request.

Trap: Fixed window double-burst.
