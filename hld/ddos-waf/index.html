DDoS protection and WAF

NEXT

Definition

Absorb or drop volumetric floods at the edge, and filter application abuse (injection, bots, scrapers) before it hits origin CPU.

Why it exists

Public designs get asked 'what if you get hugged to death.' The answer is layers: anycast/CDN, L3/L4 scrubbing, WAF/bot rules, and app rate limits.

Approach

  1. Put a CDN/L7 with DDoS and WAF in front of public HTTP.
  2. Rate-limit by key at edge and app; special-case login/search.
  3. Cache public GETs so origin is shielded.

Tradeoff: Aggressive WAF: safer, more broken legitimate clients.. Failure mode: Origin IP leaked via mail headers or a test CNAME. For Ticketmaster/Twitter, mention edge absorption + queue + limits, not just more pods.

Public designs get asked 'what if you get hugged to death.' The answer is layers: anycast/CDN, L3/L4 scrubbing, WAF/bot rules, and app rate limits. You cannot out-scale a 1 Tbps flood from your VPC.

Use when: Any public website or API.

Skip when: Do not WAF internal gRPC as your only authz.

Trap: Origin IP leaked via mail headers or a test CNAME.
