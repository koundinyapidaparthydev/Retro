Backpressure, retries, timeouts, and circuit breakers

CORE

Definition

How a system says 'slow down' instead of melting, and how retries without jitter and budgets become a self-DDoS.

Why it exists

The first outage after your design ships is often retry amplification.

Approach

  1. Set a deadline at the edge and subtract as you go; do not give every hop 30s.
  2. Retry only idempotent calls or retry with the same idempotency key.
  3. Use exponential backoff + jitter; cap retries; prefer retry-after from 429.

More notes if you want them

The first outage after your design ships is often retry amplification. Timeouts that are too long hold threads; too short multiply load. Circuit breakers and queues are the HLD tools that keep a dependency failure from becoming a fleet-wide timeout storm.

Use when: All service-to-service calls, webhooks, and DB pools.

Skip when: Do not retry non-idempotent POSTs at the LB.

Trap: Synchronized retries after a 30s outage (thundering herd).
