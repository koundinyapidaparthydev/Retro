HLD/Method

The 7-step HLD interview method

CORE

Definition

A repeatable structure for 45-minute system design: clarify, estimate, API, data, design, deepen, wrap — so you lead the room instead of drowning in boxes.

Why it exists

Interviewers grade communication and judgment as much as components.

Approach

  1. Open with a recap and a timebox: 'I'll clarify, estimate, then sketch, then dive on X.'
  2. Write requirements where they can see them; tick them as the design covers each.
  3. Drive the diagram from a user action, not from 'we need Kafka because scale.'

More notes if you want them

Interviewers grade communication and judgment as much as components. A perfect Kafka drawing that never locked requirements fails. This method keeps you on the clock, surfaces tradeoffs, and leaves time for the one deep dive they actually care about.

Use when: Classic 45–60 minute HLD loops: feeds, chat, URL shortener, rides, video, payments.

Skip when: Do not force all seven steps into a 20-minute 'design this class' LLD prompt.

Trap: Jumping to microservices before the data model exists.
