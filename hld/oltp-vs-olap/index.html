The problem they give you

They will not say “OLTP vs OLAP, warehouses, and data lakes.” They describe this:

Given: The CFO’s Monday dashboard scans checkout in production and checkout p99 triples.

Find: Split operational writes from analytical scans, and pick warehouse vs lake for the copy.

Tiny example: OLTP 4k tx/s, a 2-year aggregation, ETL every 15 minutes.

Name of the tool

Operational transactions versus analytic scans — and the modern lake/warehouse/lakehouse stack that copies data out of the primary.

Why this tool

A CEO dashboard query on the production primary is an outage waiting to happen.

Approach

  1. Forbid heavy ad-hoc SQL on the primary; give replicas or, better, a warehouse.
  2. Land raw events/files, then build clean models (dbt-like layers).
  3. Partition by time; expire or tier old raw data.
