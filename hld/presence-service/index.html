The problem they give you

They will not say “Design a presence service.” They describe this:

Given: The app wants a green dot and “last seen” without polling every friend every second.

Find: v1: online / last-seen that is allowed to be a few seconds stale.

Tiny example: 50M users, 10% online, heartbeats every 20s, friend lists of 500.

Name of the tool

A standalone online/idle/typing service: heartbeats, TTLs, watchers, and APIs other products can subscribe to.

Why this tool

Extracted from chat, presence is its own scale path.

Approach

  1. WS gateway refreshes TTL on ping; first/last session emits transition.
  2. Get reads Redis; Subscribe registers watchers and pushes transitions.
  3. Honor privacy before emit.

Whiteboard prompts

  • Heartbeat TTL vs a live socket map.

You say this first

I'd treat this as a 45-minute HLD. First I lock v1 of presence service: who uses it, read vs write, latency, what is v2. A standalone online/idle/typing service: heartbeats, TTLs, watchers, and APIs other products can subscribe to.

Then API, data, one write path and one read path. Deep dive: WS gateway refreshes TTL on ping; first/last session emits transition.. Accuracy vs heartbeat load.

More notes if you want them

Extracted from chat, presence is its own scale path. This design is ephemeral KV + pubsub + a watch API, with celebrity and privacy knobs.

Use when: Chat, collab, gaming lobbies, 'courier is online.'

Skip when: Do not offer 'notify 50M followers when I open the app.'

Trap: SQL UPDATE last_seen every 15s × 100M.
