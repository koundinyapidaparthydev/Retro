The problem they give you

They will not say “Replication.” They describe this:

Given: The primary just died. Replicas are 1.5s behind. Reads are still coming.

Find: Decide sync vs async, failover, and what clients are allowed to see.

Tiny example: One region, two replicas, RPO of a few seconds is OK for posts, not for payments.

Name of the tool

Copy data to more nodes for reads, locality, and durability — sync vs async, leader vs multi-leader, and what lag does to reads.

Why this tool

Replicas are how you survive a disk death and how you scale reads.

Approach

  1. Choose sync/semi-sync inside the AZ/region for the system of record.
  2. Use async replicas for read scale and cross-region DR.
  3. Monitor lag; fail reads to primary or serve stale with a budget.

More notes if you want them

Replicas are how you survive a disk death and how you scale reads. They are also how you serve stale data and how you lose acknowledged writes if the ack was too eager. Every HLD store box needs a replication sentence.

Use when: Always, for production data you cannot rebuild.

Skip when: Do not send strongly consistent reads to an async replica.

Trap: Replica used as the only backup.
