Design Ticketmaster

ADV
AmazonoftenUbersometimesInventory + oversell.

The problem they give you

They will not say “Design Ticketmaster.” They describe this:

Given: Taylor Swift onsale. A million people want the same 20k seats.

Find: v1: hold a seat, pay, issue a ticket — without selling the same seat twice.

Tiny example: 200k QPS at open, holds expire in 10 minutes, payment is slower than browse.

Name of the tool

A flash-sale inventory problem: waiting rooms, fair-ish queues, atomic seat holds, and payments — hot keys and correctness over cute microservices.

Why this tool

This is hot-key + inventory + abuse.

Approach

  1. Edge waiting room issues admission tokens at a safe rate.
  2. Hold: transactional compare-and-swap on seat state with TTL.
  3. Checkout saga captures payment; marks sold; issues QR tickets in object store.

More notes if you want them

This is hot-key + inventory + abuse. The on-sale minute is the design. If you treat it like a normal CRUD shop you will oversell and melt.

Use when: Ticketing, flash drops, limited drops (sneakers), class registration.

Skip when: Do not increment a cached 'remaining' counter as the lock.

Trap: Sold from a CDN-cached remaining=1.
