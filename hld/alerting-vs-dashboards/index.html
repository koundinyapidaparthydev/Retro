Alerting vs dashboards

CORE

The problem they give you

They will not say “Alerting vs dashboards.” They describe this:

Given: The dashboard is pretty. Nobody woke up, and users were down for 40 minutes.

Find: Decide what pages a human at 3am vs what is only a chart.

Tiny example: p99 crept from 80ms to 400ms over an hour. CPU looks fine.

Name of the tool

Alerts page a human for something they must do. Dashboards are for exploration and status. Mixing them is how you train people to ignore the pager.

Why this tool

Observability without this split is a wall of graphs and a noisy Slack.

Approach

  1. Page: SLO burn (fast/slow), saturation that predicts SLO burn, paging-worthy freshness (payments stuck).
  2. Ticket/Slack: capacity trends, weekly error leftovers.
  3. Dashboard: per-service RED + the three dependencies you actually have.

You say this first
